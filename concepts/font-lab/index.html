what the reels use

Genjutsu reel: Helvetica Neue, Bold/Heavy for the shock words and Roman/Medium for the connector words (double-storey a with a tail, t with a slanted cut, horizontal terminals on c, e, s). Commercial (Monotype), not free. Its free relatives in structure are Inter Display, Switzer, Schibsted Grotesk and Geist; none copy the tailed a. Krea Ad Studio UI: Suisse Int'l (Swiss Typefaces, commercial), read from krea.ai's own font files. Free stand-ins: Inter, Geist, Host Grotesk. Higgsfield's own site uses Inter and Inter Display.

considered and dropped

  • Licence: Satoshi, General Sans, Cabinet Grotesk, Clash Grotesk/Display (all ITF Free Font License 2.0, same as Switzer): commercial, web, video and staff sharing are allowed, but files may not go to freelancers, agencies, clients or printers, may not be subset or converted, and may not be offered inside a tool third parties use. That fails "hand the files to every team member's agent" whenever a team member is a freelancer. Switzer stays in the renders only as the reference for the Helvetica look.
  • Weight range too short (stops at Bold, so no heavy display cut): Familjen Grotesk, Instrument Sans, Space Grotesk, General Sans, Clash.
  • Wrong shape for the vibe: Unbounded (wide geometric, reads tech/crypto in lowercase), Onest (fine but indistinguishable from Inter at a glance).
  • Literal Helvetica clones (TeX Gyre Heros, Nimbus Sans, Arimo): free, but only Regular and Bold and they look like system fallbacks.
