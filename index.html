03

What premium looks like in our category

Each pattern showed up in two or more sources. The example screenshot is the clearest instance.

The work IS the hero

A muted full-bleed film, a tiny nav and a one-line caption (client / director / title). 28 of 64 showcase sites had video in the first viewport.

Expensive reads as absence

No sales button on the first screen and no stats. Sites with percentages read as vendors (Silverside's '68% cost reduction'). This backs the no-numbers rule.

Studio first, AI second

'An Entertainment Studio Powered by AI' (Secret Level). 'Human-directed. AI-powered.' (Utopai). Asteria barely says AI.

Provenance as proof

Human origination, chain of title, IP. It fits government buyers, and no Indonesian peer does it.

Red reads expensive when flat

One red field, one ink, one huge typeface, no gradient. Il Capo is a production company. Red-to-orange gradients read cheap.

A list that swaps footage

Production companies index work as a text list. Hover swaps the full-bleed footage (BSWT, Biscuit) or shows a cursor-follow preview (Unit9).

Red as image grade

A red-graded portrait plus a huge red extended headline: the strongest red film page.

Title card as hero

One project title at a time on a black stage, then a cut to full-bleed video.

04

The local market

18 of 20 Indonesian studio sites are English-only. They lean on logo walls, '+' counters and award badges, and none has an agency / white-label page. A Bahasa-first editorial site with no numbers would stand out. Premium ratings are judgement calls from screenshots.

05

Red, done expensive

18 red-led sites, measured. Every premium one keeps orange under 3.5%. The three palettes below use the brand red #FF3B30 with orange as a 0.8% accent.

Small white text on #FF3B30 is only 3.2:1 contrast. On red fields, small text has to be #1A1A1A (4.9:1).

Type: single faces, then pairings

16 display faces rendered with our own copy. All are free (Fontshare / Google) and all cover Indonesian. Top 5: Boska (cinematic serif), Big Shoulders (film-title caps), Clash Display (safe Poppins replacement), Zodiak, Archivo Expanded. Avoid: Anton, Unbounded, Tanker (cheap poster).

Pairings

Two studies. Measured: the fonts actually rendered on 210 of the crawled sites, by role. Curated: 19 pairing principles (Typewolf, Fonts In Use, Klim, Practical Typography, Google Fonts Knowledge), 38 in-use examples, and 14 pairings rendered with our copy on dark (A) and red (B). Every font is free and covers Bahasa.

54%of 210 award/premium sites use ONE family in every role
63%of film/production houses: one family everywhere, mostly one grotesk
65 / 66two-family pairs appear on only one site: the structure repeats, not the pair

29%of AI studios use a serif display (mostly Instrument Serif over Inter)
18%use a mono for meta/labels

The rule that falls out of both

Use one workhorse family, or pair it with something categorically different: serif against grotesk, condensed caps against a calm sans, mono for timecodes. Never two near-identical sans. Small text on red is ink #1A1A1A. Paper on red only from 34 px up.

Motion that reads premium

109 Awwwards interaction clips, frame-stripped, with 12 finalists. Motion verbs that fit film: cut, grow into theatre, title shrinks into the header. Cheap: fade-up on everything, bouncy easing, effects for their own sake.

No clip showed a clean mute-to-unmute on a hero reel, so our "nyalakan suara" toggle is a proposal, not a copied pattern.

08

Learning from motionsites.ai

A prompt marketplace for AI site builders. Useful for techniques and prompt anatomy. It is also a catalogue of the 'AI template' look juries reject, so we read it critically.

704 prompts: 274 free (full text, no account), 430 locked (not read). 305 background loops. No item has a live demo, so the five free prompts that ship code were built locally and recorded. The preview videos sit on r2.dev, which your ISP (MyRepublic) DNS-blocks, so motion on locked items is unseen.

Their prompt anatomy (we reuse it to brief our designers)

  1. Header: name + what it is in six words
  2. What it is: scene, what moves, stack line
  3. 4 to 6 numbered rules that keep it coherent
  4. Page basics: title, fonts, colour tokens
  5. Media: exact URLs, size, duration, loop
  6. Frame + unit: 1440x900 design frame, separate phone unit
  7. Layout by region: coordinates, type, colour, verbatim copy
  8. First frame and states
  9. Timeline table: trigger, element, property, from → to, duration, easing, stagger
  10. Interaction + reduced motion
  11. Responsive behaviour
  12. Bug scars (hard rules), then 4 to 6 acceptance checks at 1440 and 390

For kampanye we add: one line of positioning, territory tokens (A or B), a performance budget, the sound rule ("muted, nyalakan suara toggles") and the deny-list on the right.

The AI-template look to avoid

  • Particle brains and orbs on black
  • Glass / frosted cards everywhere
  • Pastel-gradient SaaS cards, purple gradient buttons
  • Stock chrome heads and cute mascots
  • The house formula (about 30% of the free set): full-bleed video + centred serif headline + glass pill button

Questions the designers raised

  1. Allow real film metadata (timecode, aspect ratio) as decoration, or keep none?
  2. Official title: 'Jaka Tingkir Saga' or 'Jaka Tingkir Saga Dynasty'? Series or film? May we name Dynasty Shorts?

  4. May we re-encode the 23 MB showreel for the web (about 3 to 4 MB)? Or cut a new montage across all the work for the hero?
  5. Does the orange rule cover the films themselves, or only our design colours?
  6. 'Karakter 3D' contains a digit. Keep it as the service name?
